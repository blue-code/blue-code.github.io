The Best App to Cast Web Video to Your TV

What to look for in a web-video casting app for iPhone and iPad, and where WebCast fits

If the video plays on a website on your iPhone or iPad, the simplest way to get it onto your TV is a casting browser rather than a screen-mirroring app. WebCast - Web Video to TV is one: open the site, tap the cast button, pick a Chromecast or DLNA smart TV on your Wi‑Fi, and press play. One caveat up front: it can't cast DRM-protected services such as YouTube or Netflix.

What to look for in an app that casts web video

How to cast a web video to your TV

  1. Open any video site in WebCast.
  2. Tap the cast button and choose your Chromecast or TV.
  3. Press play on the page. The video goes straight to the TV.

Once you're connected, pressing play sends the video to the TV automatically. You can switch that off in Settings if you'd rather choose each time.

When WebCast is a good fit

  • The video lives on a website that plays it directly, not inside a dedicated streaming app.
  • You want the video itself on the TV, with your phone or tablet working as the remote.
  • You'd rather pay once than subscribe.
  • You want ad and tracker blocking, desktop mode, and a dark theme in the same browser.
  • You use an iPad: there's a landscape toolbar and keyboard shortcuts.

When to use something else

  • YouTube, Netflix and other DRM-protected services. These can't be cast from WebCast, for technical and policy reasons. Use the service's own app instead.
  • Mirroring your whole screen. Screen mirroring is not included, so it won't show photos, games or slides from your device.
  • Different networks. Your device and the Chromecast or DLNA TV have to be on the same Wi‑Fi.

Quick answers

Is it free?

Browsing and video detection are always free, so you can check whether a site's video is detected before paying. Casting is free 3 times, then unlocked for good with one purchase.

Do I need a Chromecast?

No. A DLNA smart TV on the same Wi‑Fi works too.
