WebCast - Web Video to TV

Chromecast & DLNA streaming

Photo & Video · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Watch web video on the big screen. No file transfers, no switching apps — just play the video in the browser and it lands on your Chromecast or DLNA smart TV.

WebCast is a browser built around casting. It finds the playable streams on a page (MP4, HLS, DASH) and sends them to your TV in one tap.

How it works

1. Open any video site

2. Tap the cast button to pick a Chromecast or TV on the same Wi‑Fi

3. Press play on the page — it goes straight to your TV

Features

  • Auto-cast once connected — pressing play sends it to the TV (switchable in Settings)
  • Automatic video detection — MP4, HLS, DASH, including embedded players
  • Quality selection — Auto, or pick 720p / 1080p and more
  • Subtitles (CC), 0.5×–2× playback speed, resume where you left off
  • Saved videos — keep something for later and cast it straight from your library
  • Playlist casting — queue every video found on a page
  • Remote control — play/pause, seek, volume
  • Favorites and recent pages on the start page; reopens your last page
  • Ad and tracker blocking, desktop mode
  • Universal for iPhone and iPad — landscape toolbar and keyboard shortcuts on iPad
  • Easy-on-the-eyes dark theme

Free trial & unlock

  • Cast 3 times free, then unlock unlimited casting with a single one-time purchase (not a subscription)
  • Browsing and video detection are always free

Notes

  • Your device and the Chromecast / DLNA TV must be on the same Wi‑Fi
  • DRM-protected services (YouTube, Netflix, etc.) cannot be cast, for technical and policy reasons. WebCast works with sites that expose directly playable video
  • Screen mirroring is not included — it sends the video itself to your TV

Privacy

WebCast collects nothing. History and bookmarks stay on your device.
