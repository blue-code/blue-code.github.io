First to connect five wins. OmokFive is an authentic Renju-rule Gomoku game with a clean, minimal design.

Three ways to play

  • Nearby match — no internet! One player creates a room code, a friend types it in, and you're connected instantly (peer-to-peer over Wi-Fi/Bluetooth).
  • Play vs AI — Beginner, Normal, Expert. A pattern-aware opponent that attacks and defends.
  • Two on one device — pass-and-play hotseat for black and white.

Authentic Renju rules

  • Black's forbidden moves (overline, double-four, double-three) shown live on the board as a red X mark.
  • Exact-five win and overline detection done right.

Thoughtful features

  • Tap-to-confirm precise placement, last-move marker, undo, move-by-move replay, move sounds & haptics, emoji greetings, move timer.
  • Resume an in-progress game, light/dark themes.

Four languages — 한국어 · 日本語 · 中文 · English (auto-detects your device language)

No server, no account, no sign-in—entirely on your device. The cleanest way to play Gomoku face-to-face with a friend.

Gomoku Pro (one-time purchase) removes ads and unlocks Expert AI.
