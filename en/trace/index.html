Trace: Motion Art

Draw light with one finger

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Steer a dot with one finger — direction only. In 30 to 60 seconds of gathering

light and dodging obstacles, the path you travel becomes a single abstract artwork.

  • Move fast for sharp brush strokes, slow for soft lines of light
  • Graze an obstacle for a gold accent; hit one for a smear
  • End with Focus / Boldness / Balance scores and a poetic line on a vertical image
  • Share to Instagram Stories in one tap

One Daily Trace every day — everyone worldwide plays the same map on the same date,

yet each person’s result is a completely different picture.

Play and share for free. Lifetime Pro unlocks watermark-free export, extra palettes,

replaying past daily traces, and the full gallery.

Fully on-device · no account · no data collected. Everything stays on your device.
