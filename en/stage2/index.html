Stage2: Growth Stock Journal

Trend Template & Risk Coach

Finance · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Stage2 is an on-device coach to evaluate, size, journal, and learn growth-stock trading, inspired by well-known growth-stock investing methodology.

Trend Template Checker

Enter price, moving averages (50/150/200-day), 52-week high/low, and relative strength (RS) to instantly score a stock's Stage 2 uptrend against 8 quantitative criteria. Pass all 8 to earn the "Stage 2 candidate" badge.

Risk / Position Calculator

From account size, entry, stop, and risk-per-trade %, compute share count, dollar risk, and 2R/3R targets. Position limits and stop discipline are checked automatically.

Trade Journal + Expectancy Analytics

Log trades and automatically compute win rate, win/loss ratio, expectancy, profit factor, max drawdown, and R-multiple distribution. View cumulative P&L and R-distribution charts.

Learn Module

Short lessons and quizzes on stage analysis, the trend template, the Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP), risk management, and the 5-Factor Scorecard.

Privacy First

All your data stays on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.

Start free, then unlock unlimited watchlist & journal, the analytics dashboard, the 5-Factor scorecard, full lessons, and CSV export with a one-time lifetime Pro purchase.
