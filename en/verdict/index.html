Verdict: Cases

Investigate. Judge. Discover.

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

A man who could no longer afford food took some, then turned himself in ten minutes later. Under the law, it is theft. Is that enough to decide his sentence?

Investigate testimony and records, then assemble the verdict yourself — a 10-minute mystery. The philosophy behind your choices is revealed only at the end.

The first case and daily dilemmas are free · Fully offline · No ads.

You are a Civic Judge. When existing law cannot close a case, you are asked to leave behind a standard a society can actually live with.

10-15 minutes per case

Open testimony and records, put a tentative judgment on file, waver when new facts arrive, then assemble the verdict.

The evidence conflicts

You may open three of five records. What you chose not to look at stays part of the judgment too.

Argument duels

An opponent attacks your reasoning. You answer with argument cards — but nobody wins. What is left is the grain of your consistency and your concessions.

There is no right answer

It is not only guilty or not guilty. You assemble the disposition, the reach of responsibility, the value you put first, and the objection you accept.

Aftermaths show the cost and the gain

No choice is painted as evil. What was protected and what was lost are both on the record.

Philosophy comes last

No philosopher is named while you investigate. Only after the verdict are you told which principle your judgment stood near. You are never reduced to a type — no "you are N% Kant".

Fully offline

No network. No account. No ads. No data collected. Your judgments stay on this device.

Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional)

The case text itself ships in all five languages, not just the interface.

Included: 3 complete cases, a daily dilemma, argument cards, a six-axis philosophy map, and an archive.

The first case is free and plays start to finish.

The Season 1 Pass opens the locked cases, and every further Season 1 case arrives free as it is written. One purchase, not a subscription.
