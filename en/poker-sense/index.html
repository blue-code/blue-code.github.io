Poker Sense: Odds Trainer

Train your poker decisions

Education · iPhone · iPad · Free download

Top pair, so you always call? Flush draw, so you always chase?

Poker Sense is not a poker game — it is a decision and probability trainer. Like a chess puzzle app for poker, it scores the quality of your choices, not luck. Win by luck with a bad choice and you score low; lose with a good choice and you score high.

WHAT YOU DO

  • 5 questions a day, about 3 minutes. Choose Fold / Call / Raise on a real spot.
  • A clear 3-step explanation of why a choice is strong or risky.
  • 8 step-by-step courses: cards & rankings, starting hands, position, outs & odds, pot odds, EV judgment, reading opponents, bluff & value.
  • Outs and pot-odds drills.
  • Weakness analysis that tells you where your decisions slip.
  • Weekly report that turns your decision quality into a score.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

  • Quality of choice, not luck. The point is the decision, not the outcome.
  • On-device privacy. All training records and analysis stay on your device — no account, no servers.
  • Multilingual: Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese.

SAFETY

Poker Sense is a strategy-learning app that does not involve real money or rewards of any kind. There is no betting, no chips, and no prizes — only decision training.

FREE & PRO

Start free with 5 questions a day. A one-time lifetime Pro upgrade unlocks all courses, the full question set, deep explanations, full weakness analysis, and unlimited practice. No subscription.
