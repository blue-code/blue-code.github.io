PocaBox

K-pop Photocard Collection

Lifestyle · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Bring your scattered photocards together in one place.

PocaBox is a photocard collection manager for K-pop fans. Organize cards by group, member and season (album or event), and keep track of owned cards, wishes, duplicates and collection goals.

▸ Key features

  • Add cards from the camera or photo library, with optional Vision AI background removal
  • Select up to 30 photos at once and adjust each card’s classification
  • Edit and reorder groups, members and seasons, and edit card details
  • Set season goals and explore progress with member filters and Insights
  • Add wishes without photos and manage owned, wish and duplicate states
  • Export and restore backup files that include your photos
  • Free Home Screen widgets: Daily card, Next wish and Collection progress
  • Sync across devices with iCloud
  • Create collection share cards

▸ Start free

Free for 1 group and up to 60 cards, with an unlimited wishlist. Widgets are free too. No ads. Your collection stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

▸ PocaBox Pro — one purchase, yours for life

  • Unlimited cards
  • Unlimited groups
  • Remove the watermark from share cards

PocaBox is an independent app, not officially licensed or affiliated with any K-pop group or agency. You enter all group and member information and provide your own photos.
