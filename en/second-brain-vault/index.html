On-device AI memory you recall

“Where did I save that?” — now you can just ask. Save a note, photo, PDF, voice memo or link; the on-device AI titles, summarizes and tags it, then finds it by meaning later. Free for 100 saves. No account, no server.

We're all good at saving. The real question was always "where did I put that?" Second Brain Vault flips the order: you save, the AI organizes and recalls.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Save in one tap — a note, a photo, a voice memo, a PDF, or a web link. Anything, instantly, into your vault.
  • AI organizes it for you — every saved item gets a title, a summary, a category, and tags, automatically. Photos are read with OCR, voice is transcribed, PDFs and links have their text extracted.
  • Recall like a person — ask "that real-estate article I read last year," "the best cold-noodle place I saved," "the app idea I jotted last month," and it finds the right memory. It's hybrid retrieval — semantic meaning plus keywords plus recency — so it works even when you don't remember the exact words.
  • It grows with you — Insights show your top topics and saving trends, and every week the AI writes a short Retrospective of what you saved. The more you save, the higher your Brain Level.

WHAT'S INSIDE

  • Home — today's and recent items, plus a one-line AI insight
  • Quick capture — five kinds: note, photo, audio, PDF, link
  • Natural-language Recall — hybrid semantic + keyword + recency ranking
  • Insights — top topics, save volume, Brain Level growth
  • Weekly Retrospective — an AI recap of your week of memories
  • Automatic categories & tags — organized without you filing anything

PRIVACY BY CONSTRUCTION

Everything is processed on this iPhone. Because it uses Apple's on-device AI (Foundation Models), your notes, photos, voice and documents never leave the device. No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. The only time it uses the internet is to fetch the page of a web link you choose to save.

APPLE INTELLIGENCE REQUIRED

Auto-organizing, natural-language search, Insights and the Retrospective run on Apple-Intelligence-capable devices. On other devices you can still save and search by keyword; the AI features show an "Apple Intelligence required" state.
