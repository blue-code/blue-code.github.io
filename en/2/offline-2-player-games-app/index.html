If you'd rather play one deeper strategy match with a friend, each on your own phone and with no internet, try Three Kingdoms: Two Warlords. What makes it different

  • Simultaneous secret orders: both players commit on the same turn, and nothing is shown until the reveal. If you both target the same region, your armies collide head-on.
  • Structured negotiation: propose non-aggression pacts, gold, regions, generals or joint attacks. There's no free chat. The proposal itself is how you talk.
  • Betrayal with a price: you can break a treaty, but your morale falls, your renown drops, and all eight factions rethink how they see you.

How to start a game with no internet

  1. Both players install the app on their own phones.
  2. Join the same Wi-Fi network or turn on Bluetooth.
  3. One player opens a room and the other joins.
  4. If the host has unlocked the game, both of you get the full content.

Nothing is collected or transmitted, so it works on a road trip, in a cabin or on a plane, wherever your two phones can reach each other.

Which should you pick?

  • Short rounds, one device, lots of variety: start with one of the large free collections above.
  • A 20-40 minute duel built on bluffing and deals: Three Kingdoms: Two Warlords.
  • Waiting for a friend? Practice for free, as much as you like, against six AI warlords on your device.

FAQ

Can we play on a single phone?

Not head to head. Each player uses their own phone so that orders stay secret until the reveal.

Do both of us have to pay?

No. If the player who opens the room has unlocked the game, both of you play with the full content.
