AuctionNote — Auction Tracker

Rights, ROI & bid deadlines

Finance · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Stop scattering your court-auction research across spreadsheets and notes.

AuctionNote is your personal investment notebook for Korean court property auctions. Record each property's case number, appraised value, minimum bid, failed rounds and sale date, organize the rights analysis, and see your equity ROI and a sensible maximum bid — all on one screen.

Property management

  • Case number, address, appraisal, minimum bid, failed rounds and sale date as property cards
  • Filter by type (apartment, officetel, retail, land); search and sort by deadline, ROI or discount
  • Amounts are previewed as you type ("12억 4,000만") so you never miss a digit
  • Enter the failed rounds and get a suggested minimum bid

Log bid results

  • After the sale date, log what happened: failed, won, lost, new date or withdrawn
  • A failed round rolls the property to the next round with the reduced minimum (20%/30%) and a new date
  • Record winning prices and ratios; see active, this week's bids and wins on Home

Rights analysis

  • Capture baseline, extinguished and assumed rights plus your own notes; flag safe vs. needs-review
  • Bid-prep checklist (site visit, registry, deposit, loan)

ROI calculator

  • Adjust winning bid, LTV, rate, holding period, repair cost and resale price with sliders or by typing
  • Acquisition tax by home price band (1–3%), multi-home surcharge (8%/12%) and 4.6% for non-residential; capital gains tax with short-term rates, progressive rates, long-term holding deduction and local income tax (estimates)
  • Bid form check: your bid and deposit shown in digits and Korean wording to avoid the costly extra-zero mistake
  • Scenarios are saved per property

Schedule & reminders

  • Sale-date calendar and a next-bid prep card
  • 9 AM reminder the day before each bid, scheduled automatically for all your properties

Privacy first & backups

  • All data stays on your device. No servers, accounts, ads or tracking.
  • Free backup export/import to move to a new phone

AuctionNote Pro (lifetime, one-time purchase — not a subscription)

  • Unlimited properties (free: 3)
  • Max bid from a target return
  • Reminders 7 days before, on the morning of the bid and for logging results
  • Excel (CSV) export and sharing

The calculator, rights, market comparison, checklist and backups are all free.

Note: AuctionNote organizes and calculates information you enter. It does not provide official court/government auction data. All figures (ROI, taxes) are estimates and are not investment, tax or legal advice.
