HeartScript - Love Test

Romance Story & Dating Sim

Lifestyle · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Two love stories. One line from you changes the ending.

HeartScript is a romance story game where you choose what to say at every turning point — a first date, late-night texts, a confession, a fight. Some answers sound sweet but quietly wreck the relationship.

TWO STORIES

  • His Story, "First Season with Mina" — from a blind date in April to the first snow in November, 5 chapters and 34 scenes. A first kiss, an ex who shows up, and a final choice when Mina is offered a year in New York.
  • NEW Her Story, "First Winter with Doyun" — September to March with Han Doyun, an architect of few words, 5 chapters and 32 scenes. Learn to read his silences, and to say what you need instead of acting fine.

HOW IT PLAYS

  • Every scene has dialogue, inner thoughts, and a conversation that keeps going after you choose
  • Messenger-style chat scenes, and timed choices you have 8 seconds to make
  • Six stats shift with every answer: affection, trust, comfort, charm, pressure and conflict
  • The quality of your answers decides the ending. Picking the longest, sweetest line won't get you there
  • 5 endings with epilogues in each story, hidden branch scenes and special CGs
  • A results screen with your love score and what your choices meant
  • Achievements: 29 in His Story, 15 in Her Story

iPAD AND iPHONE DUO

  • On iPad (portrait, landscape or a resized window) and the unfolded iPhone Duo, the story plays on a centred stage framed by the current scene
  • Buy Full Unlock once and it opens on every device signed in to the same Apple Account

FREE TO START

Chapters 1 and 2 of both stories are free. One Full Unlock opens chapters 3–5 and every ending in both stories, forever. No ads, no subscription.

PRIVACY

No real chat screenshots needed, and no personal data collected. Available in English, Korean, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.
