PhotoTrail: Geotag & Trip Map

Map your trip, go back there

Photo & Video · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

You come home with the photos but not the places. PhotoTrail links each shot's time and location into a map of where you actually went — and when you want to go back, it gives you directions to the exact spot.

What it does

  • Your route, kept as a map

Geotagged photos connect in time order into the trip you walked. You can see where every frame was taken.

  • Go back to that spot

From a single photo, open directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps. That street you loved but couldn't name is findable again.

  • The trip, in order

A timeline with place names, so you can read the whole trip back from the beginning.

  • Location and settings per photo

Camera, lens, aperture, shutter and ISO — read them, and fix them. EXIF edits never re-encode the image, so your original quality is untouched.

  • Keep and share a trip

Share cards and PDF export with the route, distance, duration and photo count.

  • Apple Watch · Widget · iCloud backup

Record a track on the Watch and use it to place your photos on the map, and check recent trips from a home screen widget. Your trips are backed up to iCloud, so they come back when you change devices or reinstall the app.

Bonus — if you shoot a camera with no GPS

DSLR and mirrorless files carry no location. Import a GPX track from a phone logger or a dedicated device and locations are matched automatically by capture time; if the camera clock was off, correct the offset for the whole set at once. Camera, lens and focal-length stats come along with it.

Price

You can create three trips for free. Beyond that, a single PhotoTrail Pro purchase removes the limit.

Directions, automatic GPX matching, time-zone correction, PDF export and gear stats are all available on the free version. Only the number of trips is limited. Not a subscription, and no ads.

Privacy

Photos and location data are never sent to a server. Everything is processed on device, syncing goes only through your own iCloud, and there is no account to create.

Support

iPhone (iOS 17+), iPad, Apple Watch (watchOS 10+)
