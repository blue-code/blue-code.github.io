Duplicate Photo Cleaner Apps for iPhone: How to Pick One

Six options compared, the free built-in tool, and a safe order for cleaning up

The short answer: if you only want to remove duplicate photos on your iPhone, start with the free Duplicates album in Apple's Photos app, then try a dedicated duplicate photo cleaner app such as Cleanup, Swipewipe, Clever Cleaner or Remo. If you're cleaning up because your storage is full, back up to an external drive first and delete second. That's what Photo Drawer is built for, and it includes a duplicate finder.

How to choose a duplicate photo remover

  • One-time purchase or subscription? Cleaning is something you do a few times a year, so check what you'd pay over time.
  • Where are your photos analyzed? Read the app's privacy label to see whether anything leaves your phone.
  • Can you review before deleting? Burst shots and edited copies look like duplicates but may be worth keeping.
  • Do you have a backup? A cleaner removes photos. It doesn't protect them.

Try the built-in option first

Open Photos, scroll to Utilities and look for Duplicates. Tap Merge to keep one copy of each set. The album only appears once your iPhone has found duplicates, which can take a while after a big import. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, so you won't get the space back until you empty it.

Where Photo Drawer fits

Photo Drawer isn't a pure cleaner. It copies your iPhone photos to an external hard drive, SSD or USB stick, then helps you tidy up what's left.

  • Duplicate finder to reclaim space (part of Pro)
  • Storage breakdown of photos, videos and screenshots
  • SHA-256 verification: every copy is checked against the original
  • 100% on-device: no network transfer, no ads, no trackers
  • Pricing: back up up to 1,000 photos for free. Pro is a one-time, lifetime purchase, not a subscription. It removes the photo limit and unlocks AI organizing, including duplicates.

Use a drive formatted as exFAT or APFS, since NTFS is read-only on iPhone and iPad. There's more on the Photo Drawer page.

A safe order for cleaning up

  1. Back up your library to an external drive.
  2. Confirm the copies are intact.
  3. Merge duplicates in Photos, or run a duplicate finder.
  4. Look at videos and screenshots, which often take more space than duplicates do.
  5. Empty Recently Deleted.
