Photo Drawer: External Backup

USB & SSD drive, AI organizer

Photo & Video · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Photo Drawer backs up your iPhone photos to an external hard drive, SSD or USB stick and organizes them with on-device AI.

No monthly subscription — keep everything on your own drive, for life.

FEATURES

  • Unlimited queue backup — tens of thousands of photos in one go, no 500-photo selection limit, resumes where it stopped
  • Since-last-backup auto-detect — just the new photos, no dates to remember
  • Today / date range / all / hand-picked backups
  • SHA-256 verification — every copy is checked against the original
  • Browse your drive like an album and restore originals
  • Automatic trip detection (location + time)
  • Duplicate finder to reclaim space
  • Storage breakdown of photos, videos and screenshots

100% ON-DEVICE — your photos never leave your device. No network transfer, no ads, no trackers.

PRICING

Back up to 1,000 photos for free. Restoring, browsing your drive and SHA-256 verification stay free. Pro is a one-time, lifetime purchase that removes the photo limit and unlocks AI organizing (sorting, search, trips, duplicates, timeline). Not a subscription.

Tip: use a drive formatted as exFAT or APFS (NTFS is read-only on iPhone and iPad).
