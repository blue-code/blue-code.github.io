DeepRead – Secure Doc AI

Offline AI, no internet

Business · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

"Would you upload your confidential documents to a cloud AI service?"

DeepRead is a 100% on-device secure document AI assistant — it reads, summarizes, translates, and answers questions about your documents entirely on your iPhone, with no internet connection at all. Built for professionals who can't use cloud AI under corporate security policy, researchers who don't want unpublished work touching an external server, and anyone reviewing contracts offline on a plane or train.

Why DeepRead

  • No network entitlement — the app is technically unable to communicate with the outside world.
  • Summarization, translation, and Q&A all run on-device (Apple Intelligence).
  • Nothing to flag for corporate security review.

Core features

  • Document summary: auto-generated one-line summary + key points + key terms
  • Ask: natural-language Q&A like "find the penalty clause" with cited source pages
  • Contextual translation: translate selected text naturally, using document context
  • Highlight → Note: turns your highlights into a structured summary note
  • PDF / TXT / RTF support, 4 highlight colors

Privacy is the product

DeepRead collects no data. No account, no login, no server. Documents, highlights, and notes are stored only on your device.

Pricing

  • Free: 3 documents stored, 5 AI actions/month
  • DeepRead Pro (lifetime, not a subscription): unlimited documents, unlimited AI actions, Markdown note export

Note: AI summarize/ask/translate requires an Apple Intelligence-capable device (iPhone 15 Pro or later). Reading and highlighting work on any supported device.

AI-generated summaries, translations, and answers are for reference only and are not legal or financial advice — always verify important details against the original document.
