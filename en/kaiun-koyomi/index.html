Kaiun Koyomi: Lucky Days

Japanese almanac & fortune

Lifestyle · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Kaiun Koyomi is a beautiful, quiet Japanese almanac that gently tells you how to make the most of each day. It brings together traditional Japanese calendar wisdom — Rokuyo, Nine Star Ki, Ichiryu Manbai-bi (one-grain-ten-thousandfold day) and Tensha-bi (heaven's grace day).

Your birth date and plans are never sent to us. Settings sync only between your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud. No account, no ads.

FEATURES

  • Today's almanac and luck score at a glance
  • Monthly calendar with rokuyo and lucky-day markers
  • Detailed almanac for each day, with explanations

KAIUN KOYOMI PRO (one-time purchase)

  • Your personal daily fortune via Nine Star Ki
  • Lucky Day Finder: the best days for weddings, business, new wallets and more
  • Lucky-day notifications: never miss Tensha or Ichiryu Manbai days
  • Compatibility via the five elements

Almanac and fortune content is for entertainment. Your data is never sent to us (settings sync through your own iCloud).
