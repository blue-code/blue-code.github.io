StitchScope: Embroidery Viewer

Graphics & Design · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

See inside any embroidery file before you stitch it — on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

StitchScope opens PES, DST, JEF, EXP and VP3 files, shows every stitch in true color order, and converts designs to the format your machine reads. Every conversion is read back and checked: if a single stitch would move more than 0.1 mm, StitchScope doesn't save the file.

MADE FOR PEOPLE WHO BUY DESIGNS

  • Open the ZIP as it came — every design in the pack as a thumbnail, then save all of them in your format at once
  • Will it fit? Add your hoops once (4×4, 5×7, 6×10 or your own size) and see "fits", "fits if rotated" or "too large by 12 mm"
  • Thread numbers: match each color to Brother or Janome machine colors, and share a list of the threads you need
  • Print a 1:1 template with a center cross and a 50 mm ruler to place the design on fabric

VIEW EVERY DETAIL — FREE

  • Size in mm or inches, stitch count, colors, jumps and trims
  • Design check: long stitches, long jumps, empty or oversized designs
  • Pinch to zoom, show jumps and trims, rotate and flip
  • Thumbnails and previews in Files and Finder (PES, DST, JEF, VP3)

CONVERT WITH CONFIDENCE

  • PES (Brother, Babylock) · DST (Tajima) · JEF (Janome) · EXP (Melco, Bernina) · VP3 (Pfaff, Husqvarna Viking)
  • Read-back verification within 0.1 mm — the original file is never changed
  • PNG (up to 4000 px) and SVG images for listings and mockups

ONE PURCHASE, EVERY DEVICE

Viewing, design checks, hoop checks and thread numbers are free. A one-time purchase unlocks conversion, batch export from ZIP, image export, thread lists and print templates on iPhone, iPad and Mac. No subscription, no account.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

StitchScope works entirely on your device. Files are never uploaded, and the app collects no data.

Always test-stitch a converted design before production. Machine compatibility can vary by model.
