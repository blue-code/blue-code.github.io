Rain over neon Seoul. A phone call in the voice of a man three years dead.

You are the new Director of the Veil — a secret agency for the supernatural.

Night Veil: Secret Agency is an occult mystery × romance visual novel where your choices change the ending. Lead Sahyun, a shaman who draws wards; Iseo, an informant who once erased a single line from a report; and Mujin, a lawyer who hunts living contracts. Each of them carries a door they never managed to close — and together you must stand against the Correction Meeting, a ritual that wants the city's names.

Choices that rewrite the ending

Every decision moves your clues, reputation, risk and bonds. Three endings, four route epilogues and a final grade shaped by how you led.

A mystery that pays off

The Voice Ledger that trades the voices of the dead, a fire in a 41st-floor records room, and a man erased from the record. Every Season 1 thread is resolved on the final night.

Agents, cases and gear cards

Deploy agents, roll investigation checks and collect 30 gear cards (R, SR, SSR) to raise your odds. Field ops build up clues between chapters. Summon tickets are earned in the story only — they are never sold.

Rainy Seoul, beautifully staged

High-resolution backgrounds, event CGs and music that shifts with every scene. Revisit unlocked CGs and endings anytime in Extras.

How it works

Episode 1 is free. Episodes 2 and 3 are unlocked with a single one-time purchase (Unlock All of Season 1). No ads, no other payments.
