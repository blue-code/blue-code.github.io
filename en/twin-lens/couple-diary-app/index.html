Couple Diary App: How to Choose One You'll Both Actually Use

A practical comparison of shared diary and couples journal apps on iPhone, and where Dearly fits.

The best couple diary app is the one you'll both open every day, so judge each one on three things: how little effort a daily entry takes, where your private entries are stored, and whether you pay once or every month. If you want a light daily habit with no account and no subscription, Dearly: Couple Diary is worth a look. If you'd rather join a large, established user base, SumOne and Waffle have by far the most App Store ratings in this category.

What to check before you both commit

  • Daily effort. A blank page is hard to keep up. A single prompt a day is much easier.
  • Where your entries live. Ask whether they sit on a company's server or stay on your own devices.
  • Sign-up. Some apps need an account for each partner, and some need none.
  • Pricing. Find out whether the paid tier is a subscription or a one-time purchase.
  • Backups. Know what happens to years of memories when one of you gets a new phone.

Where Dearly fits

Dearly is built around one gentle question a day, such as "What were you most grateful for today?" You both answer, and a quiet streak keeps your days connected. Beyond the daily question, it offers:

  • Together View. You each capture the same moment from your own perspective, and the two merge into one memory.
  • Automatic organizing. Memories gather into a timeline, a map, and relationship insights.
  • AI storybook. Everything you save can be woven into a book of your year.
  • Time capsules. You seal them yourselves and open them on a date you choose.
  • No server, no account. There is no login. Entries live on your iPhone. Side by side, your phones connect directly. Apart, they exchange only through your own iCloud.

The trade-off to know about

Because nothing is stored on a company server, there is no automatic backup. Before changing phones or deleting the app, run Settings > Full backup. If you'd rather never think about backups, this matters.

Which one should you pick?

  • You want the most widely rated options: start with SumOne or Waffle.
  • You want privacy without an account, and no monthly fee: try Dearly.
  • You're not sure: download two, use both for a week, and keep the one you both still open.

Tips for keeping a shared diary going

  1. Pick a fixed time, such as right before bed.
  2. Keep answers short. Two honest sentences beat a skipped day.
  3. Don't critique each other's answers. Ask a follow-up question instead.
  4. Look back together once a month.
