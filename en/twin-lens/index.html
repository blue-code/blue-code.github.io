Dearly: Couple Diary

A daily question for two

Lifestyle · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Dearly is a couple's diary that connects your every day.

One question a day

A gentle question arrives each day — "What were you most grateful for today?" Answering together, you get to know each other a little deeper. A quiet streak keeps your days connected.

Two views, one memory — Together View

Capture the same moment from each of your perspectives and they merge into a single "Together View." Memories gather automatically into your timeline, map, and relationship insights.

A year later, your own book

Everything you save can be woven into an AI storybook. Time capsules are ones you seal yourselves, to open on a date you choose.

No server, no account

No login. No server of ours. Your entries live on your iPhone. Side by side, your two phones connect directly (P2P); apart, they exchange only through your own iCloud. Nothing ever reaches us.

Note: there is no automatic backup. Use Settings > Full backup before changing phones or deleting the app.
