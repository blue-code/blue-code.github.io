Three Kingdoms: One Round

Turn-Based Warlord Strategy

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

A casual Three Kingdoms strategy where every run tells a different tale. Lead a warlord, gather legendary heroes, and decide the fate of the realm.

Every run, a new tale

  • 8 random events (plague, Yellow Turban revolt, wandering strategist, tribute, harvest, imperial decree, famine, meteor)
  • Capture the enemy capital and decide the captive lord's fate — execute, recruit, or banish
  • Single combat mini-game before each battle (Charge · Defend · Stratagem RPS, winner gets ×1.10 to the main battle)

8 warlords, distinct AI

  • Liu Bei, Cao Cao, Sun Quan, Yuan Shao, Dong Zhuo, Lu Bu, Ma Teng, Gongsun Zan
  • Per-faction AI styles: aggressive, defensive, balanced, opportunistic, recruiter
  • 5 scenarios — Chaos, Guandu, Chibi, Hefei, Infinite mode

51 legendary generals

  • Guan Yu's duels, Zhang Fei's charges, Zhuge Liang's stratagems, Lu Bu's might
  • 10 specialties (charge, iron wall, fire attack, ambush, recruit, warlord, strategist, morale, rider raid, duelist)
  • Deterministic auto-battle: terrain × walls × morale × specialties

Reasons to replay

  • S / A / B / C / D grade after unification (weighted by turn, territory, generals, executions)
  • 14 achievements — Lightning Unification, faction-specific, scenario victories, Purifier and more
  • Same seed → same outcome (deterministic battle, events, and duel)

Clean monetization

  • Free. No ads. No tracking. Ever.
  • One IAP — All-Under-Heaven expansion (15 legendary generals + 4 scenarios)
