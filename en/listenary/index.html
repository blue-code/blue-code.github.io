Listenary: Transcribe & Search

Read along, find what was said

Productivity · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

You know you heard it. You just can't find where.

Listenary lets you read along with long audio — lectures, interviews, audiobooks — and find the exact moment any phrase was spoken, anywhere in your library. All of it on your device.

READ THE SENTENCE YOU'RE HEARING

The current sentence highlights and scrolls itself. Keep it to a few subtitle lines, or read the whole thing like a document.

TAP A SENTENCE, HEAR IT

See something you want to hear again? Tap it and playback jumps right there. No more thumbing the rewind button.

SEARCH EVERY RECORDING YOU OWN

Search "confirmation bias" and get every moment it was spoken across your whole library, with timestamps. Tap a result and it plays. Substring search works for Korean, Japanese and Chinese, where word spacing isn't reliable.

HIGHLIGHT AND ANNOTATE

Long-press a sentence to file it under Important, Replay, Unclear, Quote or Idea, and add a note. Review only what you marked.

COME BACK THREE DAYS LATER

How far it rewinds depends on how long you were away. Resume from your last highlight, a little earlier, or right where you stopped.

BUILT FOR LONG FILES

A three- to five-hour file is processed in chunks. Start listening as soon as the beginning is ready, and closing the app resumes from the last finished chunk instead of starting over.

FULLY ON-DEVICE

No account, no uploads, no analytics SDKs. Your audio and transcripts never leave your device. Speech recognition uses Apple's on-device recognition.

SUPPORTED FORMATS

MP3 · M4A · M4B (DRM-free)

Note: protected audiobooks (Audible AAX, Apple Books) are not supported, and the app provides no DRM removal or downloading. It is for files you legally own.

LANGUAGES

Transcription: Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese

Interface: Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese
