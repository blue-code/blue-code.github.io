What is ChartMaster?

An interactive app for learning to read stock charts from the ground up. Instead of just reading a book, you tap and answer right on real charts — so candles and patterns actually stick.

13 modules, 91 lessons

  • Module 1 · Stock terminology (FREE)
  • Module 2 · Reading a single candle (FREE)
  • Module 3 · Candle group patterns
  • Module 4 · Volume analysis
  • Module 5 · Trend analysis
  • Module 6 · Moving averages
  • Module 7 · Indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Ichimoku)
  • Module 8 · Chart patterns (H&S, double bottom, triangles)
  • Module 9 · Wave analysis
  • Module 10 · Real-world combinations
  • Module 11 · Deep-dive studies
  • Module 12 · Advanced chart analysis
  • Module 13 · Real trading setups (pullback, breakout, order blocks, liquidity sweeps)

120-question Chart Quiz

  • Endless practice: see a chart and identify what it means
  • Tricky look-alikes too — hammer vs hanging man, real vs fake breakout
  • Per-category accuracy stats to fix your weak spots

What books can't teach

  • Patterns highlighted right on real chart visuals
  • Indicators actually drawn on the chart (moving averages, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD and more)
  • Chart quizzes that test what you really know
  • Streak and progress tracking
  • Bookmarks and a searchable encyclopedia

How it works

  • Modules 1–2 are open to everyone
  • Unlock the rest by pack, or everything at once with a one-time Lifetime purchase
  • Any pack also unlocks the full 120-question Chart Quiz

Your market, your colors

  • Market examples follow U.S. trading: NYSE and Nasdaq, regular hours, opening and closing auctions
  • Candle colors match your convention — green up / red down by default, or switch to red/blue or red/green in Settings
  • Also available in Korean, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified & Traditional)

Who is it for?

  • Beginners learning to read charts for the first time
  • Anyone wanting to organize candles and indicators systematically
  • People who studied from books but freeze in front of a real chart
  • Learners who want 10 focused minutes on the commute

All content is educational material for learning to read charts, not investment advice or solicitation. Chart data is synthetic and for learning only. All investment decisions and outcomes are your own responsibility.
