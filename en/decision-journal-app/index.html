Decision Journal App: How to Choose One You'll Keep Using

What to look for, what's on the App Store, and a small daily option for days you can't decide

The best decision journal app is the one that matches the decisions you struggle with. If you want to analyze big choices, such as a job offer or a move, look for a structured app with room for reasoning and later review. If your real problem is everyday indecision and follow-through, a small daily app like Shagai: Decision Journal fits better: it gives you one stance and one fifteen-minute action for the day, then asks in the evening whether you did it.

What a decision journal app should do

Decision log, decision diary, decision tracker: the idea is the same. You write the choice down when you make it, then come back and check what happened. Before you download anything, check four things:

  • Fast entry. If logging takes more than a minute, most people quit within a week.
  • A way to look back. The value is in the review, so past entries should be easy to read.
  • Privacy. Decisions are personal. See whether an account is required and where your notes are stored.
  • Pricing. A journal is a long-term habit, so check whether it's a subscription or a one-time purchase.

Where Shagai fits

Shagai is not a pros-and-cons tool. It's built for days when you can't settle on anything. You shake the phone and four Mongolian ankle bones fall, each landing on one of four faces: horse, camel, sheep or goat. The combination gives you one stance for the day and one action you can finish in fifteen minutes. In the evening you tick off whether you did it and add a line if you want.

The result comes from the day itself, so throwing again returns the same thing. You can't re-roll until you like the answer. Over time, the ticks show which kinds of days you actually moved on.

Which one should you pick?

  • Weighing a major decision: try the structured apps in the table above.
  • Stuck on small daily choices: Shagai's one-throw, one-action format is the lighter habit.
  • Privacy first: pick an app that works without an account. Shagai does.
  • Tired of subscriptions: check the pricing model. Shagai Pro is a one-time purchase.

How to make the habit stick

  1. Log at the same time every day, right after coffee or right before bed.
  2. Keep entries to a line or two. Short and daily beats long and rare.
  3. Record what you did, not what you meant to do.
  4. Read back once a week and look for patterns.
