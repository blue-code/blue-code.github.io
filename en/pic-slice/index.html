Turn your own photos into elegant sliding puzzles

PicSlice is an offline photo sliding and tile puzzle game. Turn a photo of your choice into a square grid and reassemble it like a classic 15-puzzle — one slide at a time. Perfect for a 1-minute commute break or a 30-minute weekend challenge.

Features

  • Three game modes
  • Slide: classic 15-puzzle style — push adjacent tiles into the empty slot
  • Free Swap: tap any two tiles to swap them
  • Time Attack: solve within 60 / 90 / 120 seconds
  • Four difficulties: 3×3 / 4×4 / 5×5 / 6×6 — the grid is the difficulty
  • Three photo sources
  • Curated sample gallery (play instantly, no setup)
  • Photo Library — uses PHPicker (no permission dialog in most cases)
  • Camera — capture and play right away
  • Personal stats: best time / fewest moves / clear count, per grid × mode
  • Haptic feedback + buttery-smooth 60fps animations
  • Localized in English, 한국어, 日本語, 简体中文
  • Dark Mode + Dynamic Type + VoiceOver

Privacy that actually means something

  • Zero internet traffic. Your photos never leave your device.
  • Zero ads, zero trackers, zero accounts, zero IDFA.
  • 100% offline — works the same in airplane mode.

Made for

  • People who want a clean puzzle without ads or upsells
  • Anyone who wants to see their family / pet / travel photos in a new way
  • Quick brain workouts on the train or sofa
  • Folks who appreciate a beautifully crafted app

Open PicSlice and rebuild your memories, one piece at a time.
