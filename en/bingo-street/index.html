Bingo Street

Walk · Snap · Bingo

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

The street becomes your bingo card.

Bingo Street isn't paper bingo that ends on a desk. It's a mobile game where you fill in squares by finding and photographing the everyday sights all around your neighborhood — vending machines, corner stores, alley cats, mailboxes. A 30-minute lunch break, a loop around the block with friends — that's one round.

Highlights

  • 5×5 bingo card · 25 freshly shuffled squares every round
  • Live camera proof · no gallery photos. Only shots you take yourself
  • Invite friends · code or QR · no sign-up
  • Near or far · walk together, or play from different neighborhoods
  • Challenges · press and hold a suspicious proof, then settle it by majority vote
  • 3 difficulty levels · from a 5-minute stroll to a full-hour neighborhood hunt
  • Boards you write yourself · put your own block, office or trip into all 25 squares

How to play

1. Show a friend your invite code (nearby, or over the internet if they're far away)

2. The same bingo card is issued to everyone at once

3. The first to fill 5 squares in a row, column, or diagonal wins

4. On the results screen, see who filled which square and how, gallery-style

Where to play

  • A loop around the block with coworkers at lunch
  • A mini-game on a date walk
  • A city hunt on a family outing
  • Discovering the sights unique to a city while traveling

Boards you write yourself

The 100 built-in tiles go a long way, but sometimes you want a card made of things only your street has. "Make a board" lets you write all 25 squares and send the challenge to a friend — and your friend plays it without buying anything. Boards stay on your device; they are never published or searchable.

The game itself is free. All 100 built-in tiles, every board size and both solo and multiplayer are free; only theme tile packs and board authoring are optional purchases. No ads.

Privacy

Bingo Street doesn't require sign-up. Nearby friends connect directly over Bluetooth · Wi-Fi, and faraway friends join a room only people with the invite code can enter. Photos stay on your device and are never kept on a server. We don't even ask for photo library permission.

See you out there, in a season made for rediscovering the neighborhood together.
