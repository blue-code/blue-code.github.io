PolyMoment: Cats

Turn the shards into a cat

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Rotate the shards. At one angle, a cat snaps into place.

A cloud of low-poly pieces floats in front of you. Turn it with your finger. When you hit the right angle, the picture locks. Hints tell you a direction or flash a silhouette — they never rotate for you.

A collection of 20 cats

Loaf, sit, curl, stretch — through calico, Siamese, Sphynx. Solved pictures stay as cards.

Eight easy poses are free

The remaining 12 unlock forever with a one-time purchase (All cats collection). Unlimited silhouette peeks are a separate one-time purchase. No subscription.

No ads · no account · airplane mode

The app never uses the network. Progress stays on this device and disappears if you delete the app.
