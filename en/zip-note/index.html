ZipNote: House Hunting Notes

Score & map every home you see

Lifestyle · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

After viewing five apartments, they all blur together — "which one had the great light?"

ZipNote scores every home by the same criteria and pins each one on the map with GPS,

so you can compare your whole shortlist at a glance. No server. Everything stays on device.

Score by one yardstick

  • Rate light, water pressure, mold, noise, storage, fees, transit and more from 1–5
  • Weight the criteria that matter; skipped ones are excluded for a fair 0–100 score
  • Instant color read: green / amber / red

Location, automatic

  • GPS drops a pin when you add a home — nudge it by hand to fix
  • Walking distance & time to the nearest station, calculated for you (free)

See it all on the map

  • Every property as a color-coded pin · slide up the list sheet
  • Compare candidates neighborhood by neighborhood

Decide & share

  • Side-by-side comparison table (green/amber/red)
  • Attach photos and notes

Private & on-device

  • We collect no data. Your location is stored only on your device, never sent to a server.

Free covers up to 3 properties, all 8 scoring criteria, map, walk distance and compare.

Lifetime Pro (one-time) unlocks ① unlimited properties ② Excel (.xlsx/.csv) export

③ custom criteria & weight editing.
