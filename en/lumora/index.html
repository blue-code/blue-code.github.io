Lumora: Sleep & Focus Sounds

Rain, fire & lofi sleep music

Health & Fitness · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Lumora composes an endless, never-repeating cinematic soundtrack for your mood — in real time, right on your iPhone. Every session flows like a film score: a quiet intro, a gentle build, a peak, and a soft release, then onward. It never plays the same minute twice.

Rain, waves and fire are synthesized live on your device — never a recording, never a loop — and sit softly under the music.

  • Composed live — infinite, never-repeating generative music
  • 7 vibes — Felt Piano, Cinematic Cello, Rainy Lo-Fi, Aurora Synth, Forest Dawn, Midnight Strings, Deep Focus Pulse
  • Nature sounds — mix rain (free), waves and a crackling fire
  • Continue & Favorites — your last sound and favourite blends, one tap away
  • Mood diary — one line a day becomes a soundscape you can replay anytime
  • Mood check-in — tempo and harmony follow how you feel
  • Play-along — tap the screen to add notes to the music
  • Sleep timer — fades out gently, then stops
  • Siri & Shortcuts, Home and Lock Screen widgets
  • Background play and Lock Screen controls

Everything happens on your device. No streaming, no account, no ads, nothing collected.

Start free with Felt Piano and rain. Lumora Pro — a one-time purchase, yours forever — unlocks all 7 vibes, waves & fire, timers up to 90 minutes and the mood diary.
