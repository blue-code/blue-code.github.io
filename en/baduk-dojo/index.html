Baduk Dojo: Learn Go

Beginner Go in 5 min, coached

Education · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Never played Go (Baduk/Weiqi)? Perfect. Baduk Dojo starts with play, not study.

Quick 30-second games on a tiny 5x5 board. Place stones, capture, make territory—and the rules sink in naturally. Like Duolingo, 5 minutes a day is enough.

  • First move in 10 seconds
  • Real-time coach explains every move
  • Mission puzzles: capture, atari, ladder
  • Mountain Dojo: a master teaches step by step
  • AI matches: Lv.1–3, coach on/off

The friendliest way to learn Go from zero.
