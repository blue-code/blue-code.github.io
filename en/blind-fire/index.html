Silent Salvo

Turn-based 1v1 blind artillery

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Silent Salvo is a tense 1v1 turn-based artillery duel. You and your opponent each command a single artillery hidden somewhere on a city, forest, or desert map — but neither of you knows where the other is.

Each turn you pick exactly one action:

  • Move — slip through alleys or treelines.
  • Fire — howitzer, spotter shell, or cluster round.
  • Destroy — knock down a building to open new lines of fire and sight.

Every action leaves clues. Footsteps drift in from the north. A muzzle flash echoes off to the east. But a decoy shell can plant a false sound, and a stealth move erases your trail — so which clues you trust is what wins the duel.

Features

  • Solo vs. bot (Easy / Normal / Hard) that bluffs and hides, not just hunts
  • Pass-and-play hotseat for two on one device
  • Local network 1v1 with a friend in the same room
  • Best-of-3 matches that read your opponent's habits between rounds
  • Four munitions — Howitzer / Spotter / Cluster / Decoy
  • Commander abilities — Recon Drone / Smoke Screen
  • Strong wind bends sound clues; a shrinking bombardment zone forces the finish
  • Three maps — Downtown, Forest, Desert
  • Wind, line of sight, and building destruction stages
  • Directional haptic clues
  • Replay your last game move by move

Made for quick, satisfying matches. No accounts, no ads, no energy timers.

Hosting a local match with a friend is free for your first 3 games — after that, a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited hosting. Joining a friend's game is always free.
