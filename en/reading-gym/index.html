ReadingGym: Speed & Comprehend

3-min daily reading workout

Education · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

"If you read fast but didn't understand it, you failed." ReadingGym trains speed, comprehension, and focus together — not just eye movement.

  • Daily 3-minute sessions with characters-per-minute (CPM) and words-per-minute (WPM) tracking
  • 5 quiz types — main idea, title, summary, fact, inference — every question with an explanation
  • 5 reading modes: Pacer, Chunk, RSVP, No-Regression, Scan-and-Read
  • 5 eye-training drills: Chunk Flash, Peripheral Expansion, Accelerated Reading, No-Regression, Schulte Table
  • 200+ original English articles and 600+ comprehension quizzes across Economy, History, Science, Self-Improvement, and Liberal Arts
  • Final score = Speed × Comprehension × Focus — racing through hurts your score
  • Available in English, Korean, Japanese, and Chinese (native content in each)

ReadingGym does not promise "10x speed reading in 3 days." Instead, it builds the daily habit of skimming fast, locking onto the core, and reading the parts that matter precisely. For students, professionals, and anyone who wants to read more and read better.

All content is built in and works offline. No account, no ads, no trackers.

ReadingGym PRO:

  • Exam Reading mode with longer passages
  • Weakness analysis, premium articles, advanced eye drills
  • One-time purchase, no subscriptions
