Set a daily intention, log it

Some days you cannot decide. This app is for those days.

Shagai throws four Mongolian ankle bones to help you settle on how you will carry yourself today — and then keeps the record of it.

How a day works

  • Shake the phone and four bones fall. Each lands on one of four faces: horse, camel, sheep, goat.
  • The combination gives you one stance for the day and one action you can finish in fifteen minutes.
  • In the evening you tick off whether you did it, and add a line if you want.

What you are left with

As the ticks accumulate you can see which kinds of days you actually moved on. The monthly report is not a forecast — it is built entirely from what you wrote down.

One throw a day, and it cannot be undone

The result comes from the day itself, so throwing again returns the same thing. An answer you can re-roll until you like it is not a decision.

About shagai

Shagai are the real ankle bones of sheep and goats, used in Mongolia. That the four faces correspond to horse, camel, sheep and goat is a cultural fact; the stance system and the one-line actions in this app are written by us. The About Shagai tab keeps those two clearly apart. Nothing here is quoted or adapted from traditional lore.
