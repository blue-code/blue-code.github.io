Sokgi: AI Meeting Recorder

Korean transcription & notes

Productivity · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

The AI recorder whose recordings never leave your device.

Compatibility (please read first)

AI summaries work only on Apple Intelligence capable iPhones (iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 16 or later). Recording and Korean transcription are available on other supported devices.

Sokgi records meetings, lectures, calls, and memos, transcribes them in Korean on your device, and uses on-device AI to organize a one-line summary, key points, action items, and decisions. Everything runs 100% on this device — your audio and text are never sent to any server.

Key features

  • On-device Korean transcription with timecodes
  • AI summaries: one-line / key points / action items / decisions (Apple Intelligence devices)
  • Tap a timecode to jump straight to that moment
  • Tags and search to find recordings fast
  • Markdown / text export (Obsidian-friendly)

Privacy first

  • No data collected. No account, no login, no ads, no tracking.
  • Fully on-device, suitable for sensitive recordings (legal, medical, counseling, interviews).

Sokgi Pro (lifetime)

Free covers 10 recordings/month, up to 30 minutes each. Pro unlocks unlimited recording and summaries plus Markdown export — one purchase, forever.

Note

  • Recording calls or conversations may require others' consent under your local laws.
