MovePlan: Room Planner

Plan furniture before you move

Lifestyle · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Before you move, check whether your furniture will actually fit your new place — right from your phone.

MovePlan is a 2D furniture-layout simulator that lets you arrange rooms and furniture on a floor plan at true-to-scale proportions. What used to mean dragging a tape measure around an empty apartment now takes about a minute. See if the bed has room, whether the sofa blocks the walkway, and how your real home comes together — before moving day.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

  • Start instantly with studio, one-, two-, and three-room floor-plan presets — no blank canvas
  • Drag in beds, sofas, desks, fridges and more, then rotate, duplicate, and resize them
  • 23 furniture types including round tables, washers, and pianos — rename, recolor, or make any piece round to match your own
  • Get real-time overlap warnings so you can check walkways and breathing room
  • Work in both square meters and pyeong, with area and occupancy calculated automatically
  • Name rooms and add multiple doors and windows with door width and swing (shown as dashed arcs) to recreate your exact layout
  • Open-plan spaces without walls, like a living room flowing into the kitchen, plus floor colors and patterns per room
  • Furniture stops at walls instead of passing through, and turns to any angle, including diagonals
  • Pinch to zoom and nudge furniture in 5cm steps for tight spaces
  • Save multiple layout options, compare them, and export as an image to share
  • Import a photo of a floor plan, calibrate it to real scale, and lay out right on top

GREAT FOR

  • Anyone moving into a studio, first apartment, or newlywed home
  • Renters taking measurements while touring places
  • Shoppers who want to confirm a spot before buying furniture
  • Hobbyists who love rearranging and decorating their space

PRIVACY FIRST

Everything stays on your device. No account, no internet connection required. Works fully offline the moment you install it.
