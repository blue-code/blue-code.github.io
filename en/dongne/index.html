Dongne: Learn Korean

Real Korean in webtoon scenes

Education · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Stop translating. Start reading the room.

In Korean the same words can mean four different things depending on the face saying them. Dongne teaches the part textbooks skip — by putting you inside webtoon scenes and asking what you would actually say.

LOOK FIRST, READ SECOND

Every question starts with a picture: an expression, a gesture, a place. You work out the situation before you ever see a translation.

WATCH THEM REACT

Pick a reply and the character's face changes — warm, awkward, or annoyed. There is no hidden affection meter. You just see it.

SAME WORDS, DIFFERENT FACE

괜찮아! / 괜찮아… / 됐어, 괜찮아. / 괜찮아? — four scenes, four meanings, one phrase.

A NEIGHBOURHOOD, NOT A MENU

Cafe, convenience store, subway, school, office, home. Twenty-four episodes, 192 scenes, unlocked as you learn.

SLANG WITH A SAFETY LABEL

아아, 존맛, ㅇㅋ and ㅋㅋㅋ come paired with when NOT to use them — and with the version you say to a shop assistant instead.

REVIEW THAT REMEMBERS THE SCENE

Yesterday's expressions come back in the scene that taught them, on a spaced schedule. Context is what makes them stick.

One-time purchase. No subscription, no ads. Twelve episodes are free.

WORKS COMPLETELY OFFLINE

No account, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics. Your progress never leaves your phone — the app makes no network requests at all.
