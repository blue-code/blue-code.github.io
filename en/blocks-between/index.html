Blocks Between

Stack alone. Sometimes, talk.

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

You stack blocks alone. Sometimes, someone nearby starts talking.

A complete puzzle on its own, first

  • A falling block puzzle that feels right in the hand. Hold, next-piece preview, hard drop.
  • No internet, any time. On a train, on a plane.
  • Stop whenever you like and pick up exactly where you left off.

Sometimes, someone nearby

  • While you play, a small dot appears if someone nearby has the app open.
  • If they ask to talk you can accept, pass, or answer after this round.
  • Three minutes, anonymous. No names, no photos, no contact details.
  • If it isn't for you, leaving is always one tap.
  • At the end you can leave each other a lucky piece for the next round.

Safety and privacy

  • No server. No accounts. Messages travel only between the two devices.
  • When a conversation ends it is gone from both sides. Nothing is stored anywhere.
  • Offensive language, contact details and links are filtered on your device and never sent.
  • Report and block any time — a blocked person is never discovered again.
  • You may disclose your gender and age range if you want to. There are no profiles, no photos and no list of people nearby.
  • Anything disclosed is self-declared and unverified. Conversations are for 18 and over.

Pro (optional)

  • One purchase, forever: six themes, unlimited records, three deferred requests, three lucky pieces.
  • Conversation is always free.
