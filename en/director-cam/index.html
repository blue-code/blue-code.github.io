Director Cam

AI directing while you film

Photo & Video · iPhone · iPad · $4.99

View on the App Store

Shooting alone means no one tells you the framing is off or the audio is noisy — until it's too late.

Director Cam watches every take while you film. Nothing is sent to a server — it all runs on your device.

Script · Teleprompter

Write what you want to say; it scrolls over the viewfinder one line at a time while you record. Set the pace to match you.

Live directing

  • Video score — framing, lighting, and audio combined into a live grade
  • Face-tracking brackets, an eyeline guide, and a rule-of-thirds grid
  • Role-coded real-time coaching — Director calls your framing, Sound watches your audio
  • A live dB level meter

After the take

Total score and grade, a per-category breakdown (framing, lighting, audio, delivery), and one tap to jump to your highest-scoring moments.

Export

Save your take or share it straight out.

Privacy

No account. No server. Every bit of analysis runs on your device, offline — and your footage never leaves your phone.

Available in English, Korean, Japanese, and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).
