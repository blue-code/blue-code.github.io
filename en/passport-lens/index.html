PassportLens: Passport Photo

2x2 & Visa Photos at Home

Photo & Video · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Skip the photo booth. PassportLens turns a photo from your phone into a passport or visa photo that meets the official size rules — in about a minute.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Pick the country, then take a photo or choose one from your library.

2. The background is replaced with the required plain color, and the photo is cropped so the head (chin to crown) lands inside the official range.

3. Check the result against the rules, fine-tune if you like, and save.

WHAT YOU GET

  • 12 official specs: United States 2×2 in, UK, Schengen/EU, Germany, France, Canada, Australia, India, Japan, China, South Korea, Singapore
  • Head size measured chin to crown, the way the rules are written
  • Compliance check: face, single person, front-facing, head size, eye position, tilt, shadows, resolution
  • 4×6 print sheet laid out for the photo size, with cut guides — print it as a regular 4×6 photo at a pharmacy or photo kiosk
  • Brightness and contrast touch-ups
  • Apple Watch remote shutter

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Everything runs on your iPhone. Your photo is never uploaded.

PRICING

Try it free — saved photos carry a watermark. A one-time purchase removes the watermark and unlocks full-quality print sheets for every country. No subscription.

Rules can change and some offices take their own photo. Always check your application's current requirements.
