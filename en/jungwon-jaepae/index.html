Three Kingdoms: Two Warlords

Offline 2-Player Strategy

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Three Kingdoms for two friends, each on their own phone. Give secret orders on the same turn, sign treaties — and break them.

Solo works too: free, unlimited practice against six AI warlords.

No server, no account, no internet, no ads. Connect over the same Wi-Fi or Bluetooth — and only one of you needs to buy.

Simultaneous Orders

Each player commits on their own phone. Your opponent's choice stays hidden until the reveal. Target the same region and you collide head-on.

Structured Negotiation

Propose non-aggression pacts, gold, regions, generals, and joint attacks. There is no free chat — the proposal itself is the language.

Betrayal

Treaties can be broken. It costs you: morale falls, renown drops, and all eight factions reconsider you.

No server. No account. No internet. No ads.

The two devices connect directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Nothing is collected and nothing is transmitted.

Only one of you needs to buy

If the player who opens the room has unlocked the game, both play with the full content.

Scope

51 generals · 24 regions · 4 scenarios · 30 turns per match (20-40 minutes)

Solo practice

Learn the rules first against an on-device opponent.
