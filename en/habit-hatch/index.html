Habit Hatch: Routine Pet

Grow a pet by keeping habits

Health & Fitness · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Habit Hatch isn't a checklist. It's a Tamagotchi-style habit game: keep today's routine and an egg hatches into a little pet named Hatch that grows with you.

Drink water, take a walk, read, sleep early, tidy up, meditate, create — every time you check off a small routine, it stops being a checkmark and becomes an act of caring for yourself, and it makes the little creature in front of you grow.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Create routines — set a name, a category (health, exercise, study, sleep, tidy, creativity, mindfulness), repeat days, and a reminder.
  • Check off daily — completing a routine earns Hatch XP and lifts its mood. It only counts once a day, so there's nothing to double-count.
  • Watch it grow — egg → baby → child → teen → adult, gaining coins and stats along the way.
  • It evolves by YOUR habits — mostly exercise and your pet becomes active; mostly study and it grows wise. The habits you keep literally shape its personality.

A FRIEND THAT FEELS ALIVE

  • It breathes, blinks and looks around — stroke it and its eyes follow your finger
  • It grows a little every day, not only at stage-ups
  • It stretches in the morning and gets sleepy at night, and greets you when you come back
  • On days you keep a routine it goes for a little walk and brings back a souvenir
  • Memory album — hatching, growing up, evolving, souvenirs and routine milestones
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Picks up between iPhone and iPad automatically with iCloud

WHAT'S INSIDE

  • Today screen — a big pet, speech bubbles, today's routine cards, a completion ring
  • Routine manager — categories, repeat days, reminders, daily target count
  • Pet screen — growth stage, total XP, XP to next stage, a stat graph, current mood
  • Stats — today & weekly completion, streak, per-category totals, your top routine
  • Gentle reminders — soft local nudges at the time you choose

MISS A DAY? IT'S OKAY (recovery-first design)

Hatch never dies. Skip a routine and your pet just gets a little sleepy — the XP you've earned is never lost. Break a streak and a weekly recovery ticket can bring it back. We never blame you. You can always start again.

PRIVACY FIRST

Your records stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, and pick up seamlessly between iPhone and iPad. No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your data is never sent to anyone — including us.

START FREE, UNLOCK ONCE FOR LIFE

Free: up to 5 routines, the starter eggs & character, basic stats (today, weekly, streak), local reminders, the full growth & evolution loop.

Pro (one-time lifetime unlock): unlimited routines, all eggs / skins / themes, advanced stats (calendar heatmap, per-category history, per-routine detail), and +1 weekly recovery ticket. Not a subscription.
