Ember: Focus Timer ADHD

Body-double focus sessions

Productivity · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Focus that feels like company, not pressure.

Ember is a warm, body-doubling focus timer for ADHD brains and anyone who struggles to start and stay. Name a task, break it into tiny steps, and a glowing ember companion works alongside you with calming, on-device soundscapes. Stay focused and your ember grows.

WHY EMBER

  • Body-double companion: the felt presence that helps you begin — no people, no pressure.
  • Just start: break tasks into tiny steps so the first move feels easy.
  • Calming soundscapes: warm crackle, rain, café and more, generated and stored on-device.
  • Time-blindness friendly: a gentle ring and cues make time feel real.
  • Never shames you: ended early still counts. No broken-streak guilt, no harsh alarms.
  • Kind insights & ember garden: watch your focus grow over time.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Everything stays on your device. No account, no tracking, nothing leaves your phone.

FREE & PRO

Free forever for daily focus. Unlock Ember Pro once (no subscription) for all soundscapes, ember themes, app icons, and full insights.

Ember is a focus and wellbeing tool, not a medical device, and does not treat or diagnose any condition.
