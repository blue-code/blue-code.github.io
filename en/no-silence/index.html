No Silence: 2-Player Talk

Break the ice, face to face

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Awkward silence? Not anymore.

No Silence is a two-player conversation game for people sitting across from each other. You both answer the same question at the same time, then try to guess what the other one picked — and the talking starts on its own. Friends, family, teammates, people who just met: about 10 minutes is all it takes.

How to play

  • One person makes a room and gets a 5-letter code
  • The other enters it — you connect instantly over Bluetooth
  • Pick your relationship and vibe, then play together

Four round types

  • Simultaneous choice — match for a Sync bonus!
  • 3-second answer — reply before you can overthink it
  • Prediction — guess your partner's answer (bet your confidence)
  • One sentence — share a thought in a single line

Questions tuned to your relationship

People who just met · Friends · Partners · Parent & child — each has its own set, and your chosen vibe (light to serious) shifts the feel. Every question and answer option is written by us and included in the app — players just tap their choice, and nothing is published or shared beyond the two paired phones.

Result & share

Get today's chemistry grade from Sync, No-Silence and Prediction scores, then save a shareable card.

Offline, private by design

Works over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi — no server, no internet. Answers stay between the two phones and are never stored anywhere.
