BizCard AI - Card Scanner

Scan cards to contacts & CSV

Productivity · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Stop typing contacts by hand. BizCard AI turns any business card into an iOS contact in one tap. Just point your camera, snap the card, and the app reads the name, company, title, phone, email, website, and address automatically.

Key features

  • Instant capture with Apple Vision OCR for accurate text recognition
  • Smarter field detection on devices that support Apple Intelligence
  • Auto-mapping of name, company, title, phone, email, website, and address
  • One-tap save to the native iOS Contacts app, no separate database to learn
  • Share any single card as a vCard (.vcf)

Privacy by design

Everything runs on your device. Card images and extracted text never leave your iPhone. No cloud uploads, no analytics, no tracking, no ad SDKs. The app works completely offline, so you can scan cards on a plane, at a conference hall, or anywhere with no signal.

Multilingual recognition

Reads business cards in English, Korean, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. Perfect for international events, trade shows, and global sales teams.

Who it is for

  • Sales reps, account managers, and consultants who exchange cards every week
  • Founders, recruiters, and freelancers building their network
  • Conference, trade show, and meetup attendees
  • Anyone who values privacy and wants to keep contact data off the cloud

What you get for free

Scan and save your first 20 cards, search your history, and share individual cards as vCard — all free.

  • Unlimited scanning
  • Export your entire card library to a CSV file
  • Pick any set of cards and share them as one vCard
