Little Review: Taste Log

Rate life in 10 seconds

Lifestyle · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

The beer you had today, the restaurant you visited, the café you loved, the movie you enjoyed — rate anything in 10 seconds.

Little Review is a personal taste archive that focuses on "rating," not note-taking.

10-second logging

Category → name → star rating (half-star steps) → one line. Add aspects like aroma/taste/repurchase, tags, photos and videos (shoot on the spot), and location — only as much as you want.

18 categories for anything

From beer, whisky, wine, sake and cocktails to café, dining, dessert and bakery — and books, movies, music, games, exhibitions, travel and stays. Add or edit your own categories too.

Taste that builds itself

  • Rankings per category are aggregated automatically.
  • Places you visit gather as pins on a real map.
  • At year's end, "Best of the Year" is created for you.
  • Group your records by tags like "solo" or "date."

Couple mode (Pro)

Compare two people's ratings side by side, see the differences and your "taste match." Save and share as a beautiful card.

Anywhere on iPhone & iPad

Automatic sync across your devices via your own iCloud — no account. Plus a layout made for iPad.

Privacy is the product

No account, no login, no ads. Your records live only on your device and (if sync is on) your own iCloud — nothing goes to our servers. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Free, with lifetime Pro

Logging, rankings and the map are free and unlimited. Couple mode, Best of the Year and share cards unlock forever with lifetime Pro (not a subscription).
