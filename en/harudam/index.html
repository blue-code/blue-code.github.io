Harudam: Private Offline Diary

Voice journal, private offline

Lifestyle · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

Harudam is a private diary with no subscription — for the days you want to remember with more than words.

Write one line, add a photo, choose your mood, and keep the place that made the moment matter. Over time, your days become a calm timeline, a mood calendar, and a map of memories you can return to.

No account. No subscription. No cloud by default — your journal stays on your device, and iCloud sync is yours to switch on if you want your iPad to keep up.

Capture a day in seconds

  • One-line entries for quick daily reflection
  • Voice memos with on-device transcription — speak a day, keep the words
  • Dictation straight into the page, processed on your iPhone only
  • A formatting toolbar for headings, quotes, lists, and checkboxes
  • Photo diary for the scene, not just the text
  • Video — one clip per entry, shrunk to the quality you choose
  • Drawings and scans — sketch with Apple Pencil, scan an old letter
  • Mood tracking with a gentle calendar heatmap
  • Place and weather filled in for you — even for a day you write about later
  • Add a photo and Harudam offers to file the entry under the day it was taken
  • Tags, weather, and places for context
  • A quiet timeline that makes your days feel worth keeping

Better the longer you use it

  • Map memories — revisit where you wrote from
  • Year in Pixels — see a year of moods at a glance
  • On This Day — rediscover older entries
  • Search by word, tag, mood, photo, or voice memo
  • Media gallery — every photo and voice memo in one place
  • Templates and daily prompts for the days you don't know where to start
  • Multiple journals for daily life, travel, parenting, gratitude, and more

Bring the diary you already have

  • Import a Day One export — text, photos, dates, places, and tags. Free
  • Fill in past days from your photos — pick a date range and each day's photos become that day's entry
  • Importing the same file twice never duplicates, and an import can be undone

Own your memories

  • Face ID / Touch ID lock
  • Export to PDF, Markdown, or JSON
  • No ad tracking
  • No servers of our own — your entries are never uploaded to us
  • Speech recognition runs on-device, never in the cloud
  • Place names and weather are looked up from Apple; the lookups can be switched off
  • Optional iCloud sync, off by default — your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot read
  • App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected

Start free. Unlock for life when you are ready.

Core journaling is free to use.

Harudam Pro is one lifetime purchase. It is not a monthly subscription and it does not auto-renew.

Pro includes:

  • Unlimited journals
  • Unlimited photos and videos per entry
  • Map memories
  • Year in Pixels and advanced stats
  • PDF / Markdown / Day One-format export
  • Templates
  • Extra themes and custom tags

Made for people who want to

  • Keep a diary without putting it on someone else's cloud
  • Pair daily notes with photos and places
  • Reflect on mood patterns over time
  • Preserve travel, family, love, and ordinary life
  • Own a journaling tool without another subscription

Start with one day. Let it become something worth returning to.
