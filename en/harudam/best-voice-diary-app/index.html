All of them are free to download, so the easiest way to decide is to try two or three for a week and keep the one you actually open.

What to check before you choose

  • Where your recordings go. Voice is personal. Look for whether entries stay on your device or get uploaded.
  • Transcription. Audio is hard to skim later. Searchable text makes old entries useful.
  • Pricing model. Check if extra features need a subscription or a one-time purchase.
  • Export. You should be able to take your diary with you if you ever switch.
  • More than voice. Some days you'll want to type a line or add a photo instead.

Tips for sticking with a voice diary

  1. Keep it short. Thirty seconds a day beats a 10-minute recording you skip.
  2. Pick a trigger. Record on your commute, after a walk, or before bed.
  3. Use a prompt. "What surprised me today?" gets you talking fast.
  4. Look back. Old entries and mood patterns are what keep you coming back.
