Komorebi — Film Camera

Film looks, no AI retouch

Photo & Video · iPhone · iPad · $1.99

View on the App Store

Komorebi is a film camera for everyday moments.

Nine film looks, no AI retouching. One purchase: no ads, no subscription.

No artificial sharpening. No AI smoothing. Just light, shadow, and the soft texture of analog film.

— FEATURES —

  • Nine film looks: Raw, K-Portra 400, T-Max 400, Ektar 100, F-Chrome 100, K-Gold 200, C-Still 800T, F-Pro 400H, I-HP5 400
  • Film grain on the four newest looks
  • Photos up to 4K (3840 × 2160)
  • Adjustable filter intensity — dial each look in to taste
  • Exposure compensation for brighter or darker shots
  • Anti-AI Mode preserves natural grain and skin texture
  • Self-timer and interval (time-lapse) capture
  • Hold your phone sideways for landscape photos
  • Action Button: open the camera or cycle filters instantly via Shortcuts
  • Optional vignette, date stamp, and location embedding
  • 4:3, 1:1, 16:9 aspect ratios
  • Front and back camera
  • Your settings are remembered between sessions

— DESIGN PHILOSOPHY —

Komorebi (木漏れ日) is Japanese for sunlight filtering through leaves — fleeting, gentle, irreplaceable. Built for people who want to slow down and notice.

— PRIVACY —

Photos are saved only to your library. Nothing leaves your device.
