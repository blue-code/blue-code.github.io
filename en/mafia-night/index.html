Your phone becomes the moderator. No human host, no role cards, no internet.

Once everyone in the room has the app, a game of Mafia starts in about 30 seconds.

The app handles role dealing, night actions, and vote counting for you.

No moderator needed

The app runs night, day, and voting on a timer and tallies who lives and dies—fairly, with no mistakes or forgotten steps.

Fully offline, no server

No Wi-Fi, no data plan. Nearby devices connect directly over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi. Play on a plane, at a campsite, anywhere.

Secrets that can't be peeked

Your role shows only on your phone. Night actions (target, protect, investigate) stay private on each device. No one sees your screen.

People debate, the app runs the show

Talk it out face to face. The app is only the moderator—timer, prompts, tally.
