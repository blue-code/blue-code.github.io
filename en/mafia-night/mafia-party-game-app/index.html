Mafia Party Game App: Which One Fits Your Game Night?

Six App Store options compared, plus how to play Mafia without a moderator or a deck of cards

If you want a mafia party game app for a small group in the same room, the short answer is Mafia Night: Social Deduction. It is free to download, works with no internet, and replaces the human moderator: each phone shows a secret role, and the app runs night, day, and voting. If your group is bigger than 8 people, look at the other apps below, because Mafia Night tops out at 8 players.

What Mafia Night actually does

  • No moderator. The app deals roles, runs each phase on a timer, and tallies votes, so nobody has to sit out to host.
  • Fully offline. Nearby phones connect directly over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi. No data plan or server is involved, so it works on a plane or at a campsite.
  • Private roles and night actions. Your role appears only on your phone, and your night choice (target, protect, investigate) stays on your device.
  • Face-to-face debate. The talking and the accusing happen out loud. The app only handles the timer, prompts, and tally.
  • No data collected. No ads, analytics, tracking, account, or login.

How to pick the right app

  1. Count your players. For 8 or fewer, a no-host app like Mafia Night works well. For a larger party, check the player limits on the other listings.
  2. Decide whether anyone wants to host. Some groups have a friend who loves narrating. If yours doesn't, pick an app that moderates for you.
  3. Check the connection. Cabins, road trips, and flights call for an app that works offline.
  4. Check the phones. Mafia Night needs the app installed on every player's phone, so have people download it before they arrive.

Starting a game in Mafia Night

  1. Everyone installs the app and sits in the same room.
  2. Phones connect to each other directly. Once everyone has the app, setup takes about 30 seconds.
  3. Each player checks their role privately on their own screen.
  4. The app walks the group through night actions, daytime discussion, and the vote, then announces who is out.

Quick questions

Is Mafia the same as Werewolf?

They are the same social deduction game with a different theme: a hidden minority against an uninformed majority.

Do we need cards or paper?

No. With Mafia Night the phones stand in for the role cards and the host.
