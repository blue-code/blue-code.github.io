RunStamp: Running Poster

Turn your runs into posters

Health & Fitness · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

RunStamp turns the runs you record on Apple Watch and iPhone into emotional posters and a running diary you'll actually want to share.

It automatically pulls together your distance, time, pace, heart rate, and route, then adds today's mood and a one-line note to create a single image ready to post straight to Instagram.

You don't have to be a fast runner. A short distance, a slow pace — if you got out and ran today, it's worth remembering.

Automatic running posters

Finish your run, open the app, and a poster is already waiting. Save and share in Story 9:16, Feed 1:1, or Portrait 4:5.

7 emotional templates · your own photo as the background

Minimal Run, Night Runner, Slow But Done, and more. Drop in a photo you took today, drag and zoom to frame it, and lay your caption on top.

Beginner-friendly wording

A slow pace shows up as "Easy," not "slow," and a run with walking breaks becomes "Run-Walk." Slow runners get to show off without the shame.

Smart captions & hashtags

Pick today's mood and RunStamp completes a matching one-liner and hashtags for you.

Stamp badges

First Run · 5K Done · Slow But Done… collect badges as your runs add up.

Privacy protection for safe sharing (on by default)

Auto-hide your home-area route · blur start/end points · convert your route into line-art. Photo-background posters never embed GPS or an address.

Monthly report

This month's total distance, run count, top mood, and "sentence of the month" in one image.

Fully on device

Everything is processed only on your device. No server uploads, no account, no ads, no tracking. Health data is read-only and never written.

Import your runs and use the core posters and default privacy protection for free. Unlock Pro forever with a one-time purchase (no subscription) for premium templates, photo backgrounds, line-art, and watermark removal.
