ScreenshotZero: Tidy Shots

Screenshot cleaner & declutter

Productivity · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

ScreenshotZero turns your screenshot pile into an inbox you can clear. It classifies each shot on-device, helps you save what matters as an action, then lets you safely delete the rest.

Knows why you took it

Auto-sorts receipts, bookings, events, articles, products, chats and memes.

Save before you delete

Send bookings to Calendar, to-dos to Reminders, receipts and chats to PDF.

Swipe to clear, delete safely

Swipe to decide keep/delete, then clean up in one pass. No auto-delete. Every deletion is confirmed by you and can be undone.

100% on-device · no data collected

Your photos and the text inside them never leave your device. Zero network, zero ads, zero tracking.

Free analyzes up to 100 screenshots a month. Pro (one-time lifetime) unlocks unlimited analysis, smart classification, actions, PDF archiving and monthly reports.
