StoryLoom: Novel Story Bible

Worldbuilding & character map (iPhone · iPad · Free download)

Continue your story on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Optional private iCloud sync keeps projects, characters, relationship maps, and timelines together.

View on the App Store

StoryLoom isn't another pile of scattered worldbuilding notes. It's an on-device story bible for writers: weave your characters, relationships, events, and branches into one structure you can see at a glance — and take with you anytime.

Web fiction, novels, screenplays, webtoon stories, TTRPG campaigns, fanfic — as characters multiply and relationships tangle and routes branch, nobody can see the whole shape at once. StoryLoom makes that shape visible: characters are nodes, relationships are threads, events are a timeline, branches are routes.

WEAVE YOUR STORY IN FIVE PARTS

  • Projects — create a world (title, logline, genre, cover color) and move freely between them.
  • Characters — capture fast with name, role, and a one-liner; grow into backstory and trait tags.
  • Relationship Map — the killer feature. Drag character nodes by hand and connect them with curved threads. Each relationship has a type (family, romance, friendship, alliance, rivalry, hostility, mentor), a direction, and an optional −100…+100 affection. The thread's color tells you the bond at a glance.
  • Timeline & Branches — stack events in order and split them into branch routes for multi-ending, non-linear stories. Even tangled plots get a visible structure.
  • Export — one tap turns a whole project into a Markdown / JSON story bible. Back it up, or move it into any other tool. Your world is never trapped.

THE RELATIONSHIP MAP — A NEW WAY TO SEE YOUR STORY

See complex character relationships as a map, not a table. Drag nodes to lay them out, pinch to zoom and pan, tap a node or thread to edit it inline. Who loves whom, who turned on whom — it reads instantly from color and curve. This one screen is what StoryLoom is all about.

WHAT'S INSIDE

  • Character profiles — role, one-line, backstory, trait tags, avatar color/initial
  • Directional relationships with an affection ramp (cool tension ↔ warm bond)
  • Branching timeline with drag-to-reorder
  • iPhone, iPad, and Mac — a 3-column split layout and roomy relationship canvas on larger screens
  • Light / dark mode, a warm paper-and-ink editorial design
  • A sample world on first launch — start with a living relationship map, not an empty screen
  • Apply AI answers (Pro) — preview, then apply in one go the characters, relationships and events the AI app you use pulled from your manuscript (StoryLoom itself has no AI built in)

YOUR WORLD STAYS YOURS

Your work stays on-device by default. Enable iCloud Sync in Settings to continue your projects, characters, relationship maps, and timelines across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac through Apple's private iCloud database. There is no separate account, StoryLoom server, advertising, or tracking, and the developer cannot read your work. You can also export everything to Markdown/JSON anytime.
