VisionCut: Pro Video Editor

4K multitrack, no watermark

Photo & Video · iPhone · iPad · $1.99

View on the App Store

A full desktop-class video editor that runs entirely on your iPhone and iPad. No account, no subscription, and no watermark burned into what you export.

EDIT LIKE A REAL NLE

  • Multi-track timeline: video, audio, picture-in-picture, text, shapes and stickers each on their own lane
  • Frame-accurate trim, split, duplicate and copy/paste
  • Speed ramps with bezier easing
  • Keyframe position, scale and rotation on any clip or overlay
  • Pinch to zoom the timeline; the playhead stays centred while the cut scrolls
  • Unlimited undo, and every change autosaves as you work

COLOR THAT LOOKS FINISHED

  • Color wheels for lift, gamma and gain
  • Exposure, contrast, saturation, temperature and tint
  • Import your own .cube LUTs, or start from the built-in film looks
  • Match the color of one clip to another in a single tap
  • Chroma key with spill control, plus shape masks per clip

SOUND THAT SITS RIGHT

  • Detach audio from any clip and edit it on its own track
  • Volume curves, fades, EQ and ducking
  • Record voiceover straight onto the timeline
  • Auto Music analyses your cut and picks the best fit from the built-in tracks and anything you imported
  • Noise reduction, normalization, beat markers and one-tap silence removal
  • Multi-camera audio sync

Built in

  • 36 background tracks: vlog, energetic, chill, cinematic, emotional, corporate, travel, playful and tension
  • 16 sound effects: transitions and interface sounds
  • 64 stickers: reactions, arrows, speech bubbles, celebration, nature, everyday and studio
  • All of it ships inside the app - no download, no connection, no extra purchase

SUBTITLES WITHOUT TYPING

  • Automatic subtitles generated from your clip's speech, processed on device
  • Import and export .srt
  • Text style presets, animations, and captions that stack into separate lanes when they overlap

BUILT FOR THE iPad CANVAS

  • Timeline, player and inspector all visible at once on the large screen
  • 4K HEVC export with a background export queue, so you can keep editing
  • 540p proxies keep scrubbing smooth on long projects while exports still use the originals
  • Motion tracking, stabilization, transitions, watermarks, chapter markers and project templates
  • Still photos drop straight onto the main track as clips, so pan-and-zoom works with the same keyframes as video

PRICE

VisionCut is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase, and nothing is watermarked.

PRIVACY

VisionCut has no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Your footage is copied into the app's own project folder on your device. iCloud Drive sync is optional and off by default; when you turn it on, projects sync through your own iCloud account and nowhere else.
