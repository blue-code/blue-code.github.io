One Turn to You: Logic Puzzle

Two hearts, a move apart

The direction you send now becomes their next step.

One Turn to You is a story-led logic puzzle for one player. Jiwoo follows your current direction. Haram follows the direction you sent on the previous turn. To bring them both home, think one step ahead—and sometimes, simply wait.

TWO CHARACTERS, ONE TURN APART

Swipe to move, wait, and move again. Hold a pressure plate so the other can pass through a door. Cross fragile tiles that collapse as you leave. In chapter 4 Haram leads instead, and in the finale they move together for the very first time.

A CONVERSATION REOPENED

Return to a conversation that stopped three years ago and walk through 34 memories. Why was Haram always one turn late? The unsent notes tell the rest.

ROOM TO THINK

Undo without limit, restart, or take a hint in small steps: a question, a key tile, then the next direction. No timers or energy meters.

  • Prologue, four chapters and a finale: 34 memories
  • Delayed movement, pressure plates, doors, collapsing floors, and a role-reversal chapter
  • Unlimited undo, restart, and step-by-step hints
  • iPhone and iPad, portrait and landscape
  • Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and English, playable offline

The 8 prologue memories are free. A one-time purchase of “Full Story” unlocks chapters 1–4 and the finale (26 memories). No ads, nothing else to buy, no account. Your progress stays on your device.
