Architect Academy

Architecture design puzzle

Games · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

You're a junior architect. Read the client's brief — budget, rooms, south-facing, a garden — then lay out the building on a grid: rooms, walls, windows, stairs, columns. One tap runs a studio review that scores daylight, circulation, privacy, budget and structure with stars and reasons. Learn why south light matters, where loads flow, how to protect private space — real architectural thinking, as a puzzle.

  • Rank up from novice to world-class architect; unlock content with reputation
  • Four master styles: Palladio · Le Corbusier · Wright · Zaha Hadid
  • Special-lot missions: narrow, triangular, sloped, coastal, seismic
  • Recreate landmarks: Fallingwater · Villa Savoye · Sydney Opera House
  • Daily challenge + isometric 2.5D preview · architecture encyclopedia

No ads. No account. Everything stays on your device. One lifetime unlock.

Note: an educational game for design intuition — not a building-code tool.
