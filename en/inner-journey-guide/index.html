You are a traveler who has lost their way.

This is not a journey into the outer world — it is a journey inward. Through five worlds (Forest, City, Ocean, Mountain, Home) and twenty questions, a quiet, layered story about who you really are unfolds.

Five Chapters, One Inner Journey

  • Forest — Meet your emotions in the fog
  • City — Unconscious choices about closeness and distance
  • Ocean — Depth of intimacy and connection
  • Mountain — Who you are when crisis hits
  • Home — The inner place you return to

Grounded in Psychology

The assessment is built on validated adult attachment theory and emotion-regulation research. It places you in one of four relational profiles, then refines that into one of sixteen subtypes — with strengths, growth points, and a 3-week growth guide.

Journey Together (Couple Analysis)

When two people each complete the journey, their results combine into a couple analysis revealing attraction patterns, sources of friction, and gaps in mutual perception.

Your Data Stays Yours

All responses and results are stored only on your device. We run no backend server. We collect no name, email, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.
