Nowbox: Timebox Day Planner

Plan your day. Then do it.

Productivity · iPhone · iPad · Free download

View on the App Store

A to-do list never tells you when.

Nowbox builds your day out of time blocks, then — while you work — shows you one block at a time.

Dump it, and the day builds itself

Thirty seconds in the morning is the whole ritual. No dragging blocks onto a grid.

Set an estimate and a priority; Nowbox fits everything into your working hours and adds the

breaks and buffers for you.

It learns from your own record (Pro)

Nobody focuses on the same schedule. Nowbox learns which half-hours you actually finish things

in, and puts your important work there. That learning runs on the device — nothing is uploaded,

because there is nowhere to upload it to.

While you work, exactly one thing

When a block starts, the list disappears. One task, one countdown, nothing else.

The Lock Screen widget repeats the same sentence; with Pro, so do the Dynamic Island

and the Live Activity.

Five seconds at the end

Done or not, how focused, how hard. Three taps.

With Pro, that record changes tomorrow's plan.

An honest day

Work that does not fit is not quietly squeezed in. It is listed as "didn't fit today", with the

minutes it needs. And on a day you don't keep to the plan, the app doesn't scold you.

Completely offline

No account. No server. No network requests. No ads, no analytics.

What you write stays on your iPhone.

Nowbox Pro (one purchase, forever)

Not a subscription. Unlimited blocks, the learning auto-plan, full history and the focus heatmap,

Live Activity, and every widget. The free app still gives you five blocks a

day, the execution screen, reviews, basic stats and a Lock Screen widget — and exporting your own

data is always free.
